# Focus blocks

Working context for **focus blocks**. The first attempt worked best when the task had a clear stopping point. Keep the next iteration small enough to review in one sitting.

## Observations

Observation: people often remember the context of an idea before they remember its title. Links and short summaries may provide a better route back. ^observation

> Working assumption: a clear path back to the source is more useful than a longer summary.
